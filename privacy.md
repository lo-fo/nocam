# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

This policy explains what the nocam app ("the app", "we") does with your information.
The short version: there are no accounts, no names, no emails, no analytics and no
ads. Clips you save are uploaded so you can share them, and anyone you give a clip's
link to can watch it.

## No account, no profile

The app never asks who you are. When it first runs, it creates a random identifier
on your phone. It names the folder your clips are uploaded to, and that is all it is
used for. It is not linked to your name, phone number, email, Apple account or device
identifiers.

## Camera and microphone

- **While the app is open**, it keeps the last few moments of video and sound from
  the camera and microphone in your phone's memory, so a clip can start a little
  before you press the button. Anything you do not save is overwritten and never
  leaves your phone.
- **When you press the button**, the moments around it are saved as a clip. Nothing
  is recorded while the app is closed.

## Clips you save

- **On your phone.** Saved clips are kept in the app's own storage, and are visible
  in the Files app under nocam. The app lists a clip for 72 hours after it is
  recorded.
- **Uploaded.** Each saved clip, video and sound, is uploaded to our storage at
  Cloudflare R2, in two qualities so it plays well on slow connections.
- **Public by link.** A clip plays in any browser from its link. **Anyone who has a
  clip's link can watch it.** Links contain random identifiers and are not listed
  anywhere, so a clip is only seen by people the link reaches. Only share links with
  people you are happy to have them.
- **Deletion.** Uploaded clips are not yet deleted automatically from our storage.
  To have a clip deleted, email us its link and we will delete it.

## Photo library

The app only adds to your photo library, and only when you choose to save a clip
there. It never reads your photos.

## Servers and network

Uploads and playback go through a small server we run on Cloudflare Workers, and
clips are stored at Cloudflare R2. As with any internet service, Cloudflare sees
the IP address of your phone and of anyone watching a clip. We do not use it to
identify you, build profiles or track you.

## What we don't do

- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
- No advertising, and no data shared with advertisers or data brokers.
- No selling of any data.
- No access to your contacts, location or photo library contents.

## Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
anything from them. If you believe a clip shows a child and should not be up,
contact us and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you may have
rights to access, correct or delete your data. Since there is no account, the way to
reach your data is a clip's link: send it to us and we will delete that clip.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
