# Support

Need help with nocam, or want a clip taken down? Email
[hi@lo.fo](mailto:hi@lo.fo). It's one person on the other end, so replies may take
a few days.

When you write, include:

- your phone model and iOS version,
- what you did and what happened,
- a screenshot if you have one.

## Taking a clip down

Email us the clip's link and we will delete it. For anything urgent, such as a clip
of you shared without your consent or a clip of a child, say so in the subject and
we will deal with it first.

## Common questions

**Who can watch my clips?**
Anyone who has a clip's link. Links are not listed anywhere, so only the people you
share a link with, and anyone they pass it on to, can watch it.

**Why did a clip disappear from the app?**
The app lists a clip for 72 hours after it is recorded. To keep one, save it to your
photo library before then.

**The share button shows a cloud instead.**
The clip is still uploading. It can be shared once enough of it is up to play.

**I deleted the app. Can I get my clips back?**
Not from the app: there is no account, so nothing links a new install to your old
clips. Any links you shared keep working.
